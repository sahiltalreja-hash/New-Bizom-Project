Area Creation & Assigning

Areas sit between your warehouse/distributor and your beats, get this layer right and beat planning, scheme assignment and reporting all fall into place.

3 min read
Web & MDM
Beginner friendly
On this page
  1. Create an area
  2. Area MDM templates
  3. Which template should you use?

1. Create an area in the geography master

  1. Open the Add Area form

    Go to yourcompanyurl.bizom.in/areas/add

  2. Fill in the details

    FieldWhat to enter
    Area NameName of the area you want to create.
    Zone / SubzoneThe zone or subzone this area belongs to.
    Warehouse / DistributorThe warehouse assigned to this area.
    ERP IDOptional, only if your system uses ERP integration.

  3. Submit the form

    The new area is saved and immediately available for beat mapping and outlet assignment.

TipCreating a lot of areas at once? Use the MDM bulk upload below instead of adding them one by one.

2. Area MDM templates

Four MDM templates cover everything related to area management:

#TemplatePurpose
1AreasUpload new areas in bulk.
2Area UpdateUpdate an existing area's warehouse mapping and active status.
3Beat Area MappingMap beats to areas.
4Transfer AreasTransfer areas within a DSM zone.
