How to navigate to Orders & Payments

  1. Log in to the portal

    Go to your Bizom web portal URL and sign in with your admin credentials.

  2. Open the main menu

    Look for Orders & Payments in the top navigation bar, or go directly to /orders and /payments.

  3. Click into Orders & Payments

    This opens the section with its sub-sections below.

What's inside

Sub-sectionWhat it's for
Secondary OrdersView, filter, approve, reject and fulfil secondary orders (retailer → distributor).
Primary OrdersView and manage replenishment orders to distributors.
Direct SalesOrders placed directly to outlets.
