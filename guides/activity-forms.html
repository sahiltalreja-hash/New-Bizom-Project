What is an Activity Form?

An Activity Form is a structured form used to capture specific activities performed during an outlet visit, order taking, stock checking, merchandising audits, competitor tracking, and more. Unlike a Generic Form, it's tied to the outlet visit itself.

Before you startYou'll need portal admin rights, a clear idea of the activity you want to track, the target role/designation, and the Outlet Visit Workflow must be enabled for the form to appear on the app.

Steps to create an Activity Form

  1. Navigate to Forms

    Go to Admin Panel → Settings → Forms → Activity Forms.

  2. Click "Add Form"

    Use the + Add or Create Form button at the top right.

  3. Fill in form details

    FieldWhat to enterRequired
    Form NameName of the activity form✅ Yes
    Form TypeSelect "Activity"✅ Yes
    Outlet EntitySelect outlet Types or Categories to restrict to—
    Activity TypeWhen the form is filled, Start Call / During Call / End Call✅ Yes
    Active StatusActive or Inactive✅ Yes

  4. Add form fields

    FieldWhat it means
    Field LabelDisplay name of the field
    Field TypeText, Number, Dropdown, Date, Image, Checkbox, Radio, Barcode, etc.
    MandatoryYes / No
    SequenceThe order fields appear in
    Conditional LogicShow/hide based on another field's value

  5. Assign the form

    To the relevant Role (e.g. Salesman, ASM, RSM), Designation, User, or Warehouse.

  6. Save & publish

    It appears on the mobile app for assigned users during outlet visits.

  7. Verify on the app

    Start an outlet call as a test user and confirm the form appears at the configured execution point.

TipActivity Forms can be made mandatory, users can't complete the outlet visit without filling them.

NoteActivity Forms are tied to the outlet visit workflow. If a form isn't showing up, confirm that workflow is enabled for that user.
