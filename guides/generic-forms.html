What is a Generic Form?

A Generic Form is a flexible, customisable form used to capture any type of field data during an outlet visit or a standalone activity, surveys, feedback, audits, competitor data and more.

Before you startYou'll need portal admin rights, a clear idea of what data you want to capture, and to know which role or designation will fill it in.

Steps to create a Generic Form

  1. Navigate to Forms

    Go to Admin Panel → Settings → Forms → Generic Forms, or yourcompanyurl.bizom.in/settings/genericform.

  2. Click "Add Form"

    Use the + Add or Create Form button at the top right.

  3. Fill in form details

    FieldWhat to enterRequired
    Form NameName of the form✅ Yes
    Form TypeSelect "Generic"✅ Yes
    Active StatusActive or Inactive✅ Yes

  4. Add form fields

    For each field you want to capture:

    FieldWhat it means
    Field LabelThe display name of the field
    Field TypeText, Number, Dropdown, Date, Image, Checkbox, Radio, etc.
    MandatoryYes / No
    SequenceThe order fields appear in

  5. Assign the form

    Assign it to the relevant Role (e.g. Salesman, ASM), Designation, or specific User.

  6. Save & publish

    Click Save to publish, it appears on the mobile app for assigned users.

  7. Verify on the app

    Ask a test user to log in and confirm the form appears under the Generic Forms section.

TipYou can add conditional logic to fields, show or hide a field based on another field's response.

NoteGeneric Forms only appear on the mobile app after the Generic Form workflow is enabled for a user, from the User Edit page.
