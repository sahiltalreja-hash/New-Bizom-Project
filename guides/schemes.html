Schemes

Schemes are the promotional offers and discounts you run to drive sales, liquidate stock and incentivise purchases, from a simple "buy 10 get 1 free" to multi-slab, payment-linked offers.

1. What are schemes?

A scheme is a promotional offer or discount a brand extends to warehouses, distributors, retailers or consumers to drive sales, liquidate stock or incentivise purchases. As part of a scheme, buyers can receive:

  • A discount on the same product, or a different one
  • Free products once a purchase condition is met
  • Cashback

Scheme levels

Schemes can be configured at two levels:

  • Primary schemes: applied when a distributor purchases from the warehouse.
  • Secondary schemes: applied when a retailer purchases from the distributor.

Scheme types

TypeEligibility is based on…Example
Quantity basedUnits of product purchasedBuy 10 units, get 1 free / 5% off / ₹B cash discount
Value basedTotal order value (₹)Order above a set amount, get a % discount or free units
Lines per callNumber of distinct SKUs (lines) soldBuy 2 lines, get ₹1 off plus 1% extra discount
Payment modeHow the retailer/distributor paysPay via UPI or wallet, get 1% cashback

2. Quantity based schemes

Quantity based schemes trigger a benefit once a certain number of units is purchased. The most common ones are Free SKU, Free Cash and Discount (%) schemes.

Creating a quantity based scheme

  1. Start a new scheme

    From Discounts, select New Scheme, or go to http://<domainname>.bizom.in/schemes/addnew. Quantity based is selected by default.

  2. Fill in the scheme details

    FieldWhat it means
    NameA name users will recognise the running scheme by.
    ProductThe products the scheme applies to.
    QuantityUnits that must be purchased to trigger the scheme.
    Save Cash / Max CashAmount saved, and the cap a salesman can offer if given edit rights.
    Scheme End DateMandatory, when the scheme stops applying.
    Discount (%)e.g. entering 20 gives the outlet a 20% discount.
    QPDS (From/To)Quantity Per Display Scheme, a time-boxed variant, e.g. "sell ₹10,000 within 3 days, get ₹1,000 off".
    Unit case calculation / Apply in caseControls whether the quantity is evaluated in units or in cases.
    Free SKU / Free Qty / Max QtyWhich product is given free, how much, and the per-purchase cap.
    CeilingMaximum free SKUs the scheme will ever give out (0 = unlimited).

  3. Click Add Scheme, then Save all

    You'll see "Schemes saved successfully".

Common quantity-based scheme patterns

Free SKU schemes

PatternExample
Free SKU on quantity purchasedBuy ≥2 units, get 1 SKU free
Multiple free SKUsBuy ≥2 units, get SKU-1 and SKU-2 free
With a ceiling2+1 scheme with ceiling 2, buying 100 units still caps the free SKUs at 2

Free Cash schemes

PatternExample

Discount (%) schemes

PatternExample

Editing a scheme

Go to Discounts → Schemes (or /schemes/index), click Edit against the scheme, make your changes, then click Save all.

3. Value based schemes

Value based schemes work the same way as quantity based schemes, except eligibility is based on order value rather than units purchased.

  1. Start a new scheme and switch to "Value based"

    From Discounts → New Scheme, click the Value based option.

  2. Fill in the same style of fields

    Name, Product, For Amount (the order value threshold), Save Cash, Max Cash, Scheme End Date, Discount %, QPDS, Free SKU details and Ceiling all work the same as quantity based schemes.

  3. Add Scheme, then Save all

NoteValue based Free SKU / Free Cash / Discount % schemes follow the same patterns as quantity based ones, just triggered by order value instead of units. For example: "spend ≥ ₹200 on an SKU, get 1 free."

4. Multi / slab schemes

Multi schemes let you configure several purchase slabs with different benefits under one scheme, e.g. "buy SKU A, get ₹10 off at 5 units, ₹25 off at 10 units."

  1. Go to https://<DomainName>.bizom.in/schemes/addscheme

  2. Pick a Scheme Type

    Quantity based, Value based, Cash/SKU, or Lines per call.

  3. Set Apply Type to "Slab"

  4. Fill in the scheme header

    Scheme name, ERP ID, start/end dates, and whether it applies per unit, per case, or per kg.

  5. Configure each slab under Scheme Benefits

    Slab name, quantity threshold, benefit SKUs, save cash / max cash, discount %, slab start/end dates, free SKU details and ceiling, then click Add Slab and repeat for each additional slab.

  6. Click Save

    You'll see "Scheme saved successfully", with a prompt to assign the scheme right away.

5. Assigning schemes

A scheme isn't visible to anyone until you assign it. Schemes assigned to a warehouse appear in primary ordering; schemes assigned to an outlet appear in secondary ordering.

  1. Go to Discounts → Assign Schemes

    Or http://<DomainName>.bizom.in/schemes/assignscheme

  2. Pick the scheme and the zone

  3. Choose the Holder Type

    Warehouse, Distributor, or Outlet, then drill down to the specific Warehouse → Distributor → Depo → Area → Outlet as required.

  4. Click Assign Scheme

    You'll see "Scheme Assigned Successfully". Use List Scheme holders (under More) to review every holder a scheme is assigned to.

For a multi scheme specifically, use Multiple Group in the assign flow to map several holders at once.

What the sales team sees

Once assigned, schemes show up right in the order flow. Tapping the scheme icon against a product reveals the offer, and the Add Order screen shows exactly what's earned free or saved.

6. Scheme MDM templates

Setting up dozens of schemes at once? Four MDM templates cover bulk scheme management:

TemplatePurpose
Scheme Upload / Discount UploadCreate scheme groups and schemes in bulk.
Scheme Holder Upload / Discount Holder UploadAssign existing schemes to holders (warehouses, zones, outlets, etc.).
Scheme Budget UploadUpload budget limits per scheme holder.
FreeSkuCappingSet free SKU limits per distributor, per scheme.

  1. Navigate to MDM

    Search "scheme" in the MDM listing to see every scheme-related template available on your instance, at yourcompanyurl.bizom.in/schemes/addscheme.

  2. Download the template

    It includes all required column headers, pre-filled for your instance.

  3. Fill in the template

    Common columns: scheme_name, scheme_erp_id, schemegroup_name, schemetype, startdate, enddate, min_qty/min_value, benefit_type, benefit_value, sku_id/sku_name.

  4. Upload the file

    Go back to the MDM page, click Upload, select your file and submit.

  5. Verify the upload

    Check the status log, errors are shown row by row; successful rows create or update scheme records.

  6. Confirm in Schemes

    Check Scheme Groups and Scheme Holders to confirm everything landed correctly.

WarningAlways use the downloaded template, don't build columns from scratch. Exact column names are specific to your Bizom instance.

🔍 Search "scheme", your instance may have several variants 📅 Match date formats exactly 🆔 Use Bizom system IDs, not ERP codes, unless configured otherwise 🔄 Re-uploading updates existing records, doesn't duplicate 💰 Upload the budget MDM after holder assignment is complete
